# Using quotations and test drives to make the final choice

**Created:** 10 November 2026. This is a process for a first-time car buyer comparing petrol, battery-financed EV and hybrid choices in Kolkata. It intentionally avoids converting subjective judgments into arbitrary numerical scores.

## Decision has two independent dimensions

**Financial:** the same five-year economic cost calculation and assumptions, with actual dealer prices and contract-specific battery details replacing generic guesses. Show the monthly equivalent and uncertainties.

**Qualitative:** personal driving experience, suitability for family members, ergonomics, ease of ownership, servicing, build, safety documentation and warranty. Keep separate **positive points**, **negative points** and **unanswered questions** for every named car.

A car being ₹2,000/month cheaper does not automatically make it the right choice; equally, a comfortable test drive should not hide a significant contractual liability.

## Suggested test-drive notes per car (use specific observations)

- **Drive:** visibility, turning radius, city traffic response, steering, brakes, suspension on rough roads, regenerative braking behavior, noise, low-speed manoeuvres and parking.
- **Comfort:** front and rear seating, legroom, back support, air conditioning, getting in/out, child/elderly ease, boot practicality.
- **Quality and controls:** visibility of displays, usability of controls, unwanted alerts, materials, rattles, headlight performance.
- **Ownership:** service centre convenience, waiting times, booking, real parts availability, included warranties, spare tyre, tyre replacement cost.
- **EV-specific:** home charging access, delivered charger/installation, efficiency, public charging support, battery warranty for second owners, battery loan terms if financed.
- **Hybrid-specific:** strong hybrid vs plug-in hybrid, real city economy, whether petrol mileage includes battery assist, battery warranty, lack/presence of charging requirement.
- **Costs:** exact variant and all optional packages, on-road price breakup, insurance first-year inclusion, loan and processing fees, extended warranty, exchange/discount offers.

For every positive or negative, state whether it is a **first-hand test-drive impression**, **dealer statement**, **third-party review**, or **official specification**. Review quotes or source claims should be accurately attributed.

## Quote status and uncertainty

A typical record evolves as:
`Research` → `Quoted` → `Test driven` → `Shortlisted` (or `Rejected`).

This is descriptive, not a standardized rating system. If fuel efficiency, resale value or servicing is unverified, the site will still calculate with inherited template assumptions, clearly showing how many parameters were actually provided.

## Questions that must be resolved before commitment

- Is the written on-road price for exactly the same variant and city and the same coverage?
- Are battery, charger, installation, taxes, registration, accessories and insurance included or separately financed?
- Under BaaS, who owns the battery, what is the actual **fixed EMI and term**, and what remains due at resale?
- Are the used-car resale estimates credible after five years and roughly 73,000 km?
- What repair, battery, insurance and warranty obligations are likely to remain with a future second owner?
- Which negative test-drive observations are dealbreakers, even if the cost is low?

## Updating the shortlist

Follow [adding-car-quotes.md](adding-car-quotes.md) and edit [data/cars.json](../data/cars.json). New records should capture both financial inputs and prose notes. Do not manufacture positives or negatives for cars that have not been driven. Keep exact user quotations private unless permission is given to publish a redacted version in this **public** repository.

## Final discussion format

For each finalist: **(1)** full economic monthly cost; **(2)** how much of that estimate relies on quotes vs assumptions; **(3)** strongest personal positives; **(4)** strongest negatives and open questions; **(5)** whether those concerns can be resolved with further research or a second test drive.

Avoid pretending there is a scientifically precise 'weighted total score' for subjective comfort or design unless the family deliberately chooses such a scoring system.
